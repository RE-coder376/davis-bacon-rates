Davis-Bacon wage rates, by county

Prevailing wage rates for federal construction contracts, by county. 2,939 active general wage determinations, 2,281 counties, 80,442 classifications — each with its base rate and fringe, and the determination number and date beside it. Free, no sign-up.

    A county has several determinations at once — Building, Residential, Highway and Heavy — and their rates are not the same. Every county page here shows all of them, because choosing the wrong construction type is a common reason a certified payroll is rejected.
